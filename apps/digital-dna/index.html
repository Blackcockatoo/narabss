🧭 How to play

A quick guide to the four things you can do.

1. Pick a colour

Tap Red, Black or Blue at the bottom. Each one makes a different set of colours and sounds.

2. Choose a view

Try Spinning Spiral, Colour Mandala or Dot Storm. Each view shows the same number pattern in a different way.

3. Play with the dots

In Dot Storm, move your mouse and the dots will follow, bounce and connect. Energy makes them react more.

4. Paint the mandala

In Colour Mandala, click and drag to draw your own colour marks on top of the pattern.

About DNA Pattern Art

Three 60-number sequences (Red, Black, Blue) turn into coloured dots, spinning spirals, mandalas and sounds. Pick your colour, paint on the mandala, move your mouse through the dots, and tap the bars to hear the notes. Dad-built, kid-powered.
