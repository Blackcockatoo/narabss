# Baby B$S Design Rules

Same Blue Snake Studios DNA, softened into a child-safe colouring-book arcade.

## Keep

- black ink line art
- cream paper
- blue snake accents
- friendly creatures
- rounded shapes
- sticker-style buttons
- soft neon arcade details
- printable page energy

## Avoid

- horror
- gore
- weapons
- adult occult symbols
- private child details
- school or location details
- public child contact forms

## Test

Could this be printed as a colouring page, used as a website card, and shown proudly to family without explaining the heavy lore?

If yes, it is Baby B$S.
